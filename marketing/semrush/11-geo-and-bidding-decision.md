## Impressions vs Conversions → CONVERSIONS, permanent rule

The account already ran this experiment both ways:
- Impression buying (old campaign, Display expansion): 39.4K impr, CTR 0.59%,
  $8.04 search CPC, 11 dubious conversions, dead landing.
- Conversion buying (C1): CTR 12.7%, 4 leads @ $42 CPA in week one.

In a niche of hundreds-of-searches/month, maximizing impressions = buying tourist/job
junk. All campaigns: **Maximize Conversions**, no tCPA until post-learning. If a
low-volume campaign can't exit learning → temporary Maximize Clicks with tight
negatives. Never impression-share bidding, never Display. Impression share stays a
DIAGNOSTIC (target ≥60% on C2), not an objective.
