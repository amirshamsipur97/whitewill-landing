## The lesson from both campaigns
Conversion bidding works in BOTH (CPA ~$40 in both) — the variable is WHERE the
volume comes from. Old campaign bought volume from Display (cheap, low intent,
unverifiable quality). C1 buys real search intent but in a tiny pond (AE).
The strategy: keep conversion bidding, move the budget to ponds with real fish.
