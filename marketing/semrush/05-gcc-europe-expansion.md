Data pulled live via Semrush MCP (June 2026 volumes). Goal: explain low AE
impressions and pick the highest-impression next market.

The AE inventory is structurally tiny. Monthly search volumes (ae database):

|---|---|
| buy property in oman | 20 |
| oman real estate | 170 |
| invest in oman | 50 |

| oman golden visa | <10 (not even listed) |

The whole EN cluster in AE is ~400–600 searches/month ≈ 15–20/day. With close
variants, 804 impressions in 6 days (~130/day) means the campaign is already
capturing MOST of the available demand. Nothing is broken; the pond is small.

## 3. Verdicts

**GCC winner: OMAN itself (om geo), by an order of magnitude.** Residents +
expats inside Oman generate 10x the AE demand, at CPCs $0.20–1.37 (vs AE
avg $2.39 actual). Saudi/Qatar/Kuwait/Bahrain are all AE-sized or smaller —
not worth separate campaigns.

**Europe winner: UK** (biggest EN volumes: golden visa 90, real estate 170,
buy property 40; CPC $0.5–1.35). Distant #2: **Germany but ONLY in Persian** —
«خرید ملک در عمان» 70/mo (Iranian diaspora), plus the fa cluster (اقامت عمان,
ثبت شرکت در عمان) — pair with the existing 4-lang site (fa pages ready).

**Cheap mass impressions: India + Pakistan** (in: 210+90, pk: 170+50, CPC
$0.22–0.35). Highest impression-per-dollar but mixed lead quality
(job-seekers vs investors) — the current search terms already show
"oman investor visa for pakistani" style demand. Optional volume play.

## 4. Recommended rollout (max impressions, protect current learning)

1. **NEW Campaign C2: "OM | EN | Oman Property & Investment"** — clone of C1
   structure, geo=Oman (presence), same 3 ad groups + `invest in oman`,
   `oman investor visa`, `real estate companies in oman`,
   `apartments for sale in muscat` promoted to their own tight groups.
   Budget $15–20/day. Expected: 5–10x the impressions of AE at ~1/5 the CPC.
2. **C3: "UK | EN | Golden Visa & Buy Property"** — revive the old paused UK
   intent (fix its 404 landing first): golden-visa + buy-property groups.
3. **C4: "DE+EU | FA | اقامت و ملک عمان"** — Persian ads targeting Germany
   (+ optionally UK/NL/SE), landing on the fa pages.
4. Leave AE C1 untouched until learning completes (~Jul 17), then trim
   sovereign-fund negatives.
