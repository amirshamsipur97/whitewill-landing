## 1. This session: being found by Google's AI Mode, not just by Google

The owner sent a screenshot of the AI Mode answer for «خرید ملک در عمان». Read
it correctly before doing more work here, because the obvious reading is wrong.

**We were already winning the part that is hard.** The answer renders our /buy
page as a source card and attaches an "Irfan Investment Group" citation chip to
its most important paragraph, the Freehold and ITC warning. Google accepted this
site as the authority for the ownership rule.

**We lost the part that is easy.** Section 3 of that answer, «وب‌سایت‌های
تخصصی و کارگزاری‌های فارسی‌زبان», names Homelist, OmanMelk and Imtilak Global.
Not us.

🔑 **Root cause, verified by grep before any code was written: the phrase
«فارسی‌زبان» appeared ZERO times in `src/`.** Not once, in a site with a
complete Persian edition. A model cannot place a business in a category no text
claims it belongs to. We were legible as "a source of Oman property prices" and
illegible as "a Persian-speaking brokerage".

This is an entity problem, not a crawl, index or authority problem. Do not
attack it with more keyword pages.

## 2b. The fa cannibalization: fixed at the root, second pass same day

The owner sent a SERP screenshot showing the Pakistani guide still ranking for
«خرید ملک در عمان» and asked why it was not fixed.

🔑 **It was fixed. The screenshot was a stale index entry, and the proof is in
the title.** Live title: «راهنمای پاکستانی‌ها برای **سرمایه‌گذاری در املاک**
عمان ۲۰۲۶». SERP title: «راهنمای پاکستانی‌ها برای **خرید ملک در عمان** ۲۰۲۶»,
which is the pre-08-23 string. Google had not recrawled in the two days since.
**Always diff the live `<title>` against the SERP title before believing a
"still broken" SEO report.** It separates "not fixed" from "not recrawled" in
one command and it costs nothing.

On-page there was nothing left to win, measured on prod that day:

| | PK page | fa pillar |
|---|---|---|
| «خرید ملک در عمان» mentions | 5 | 17 |
| in headings | 0 | 5 |
| in `<title>` | no | yes |
| inbound internal links from fa pages | 1 | 18 |

**So the remaining problem was structural.** The 4-language generator had
produced PERSIAN editions of nationality guides whose audience does not read
Persian: `buy-property-oman-pakistani-investors-guide-2026` (126),
`can-indians-buy-property-oman-nri-guide-2026` (105) and
`buy-property-salalah-indian-investors-nri-2026` (123). Pakistanis read Urdu
and English, Indian NRIs read English. Each served nobody while competing with
the pillar for the site's most valuable Persian query. Retitling them one at a
time is whack-a-mole, which is what the 08-17 and 08-23 rounds were.

Fix, per fa row: **unpublish AND 301.** Both halves are required.
- The 301 (in `vercel.json`, `/fa/` shape only) folds the accrued signal into
  the right page instead of discarding it, and is reversible.
- The unpublish is what keeps it honest: `api/sitemap.js` and
  `prerender-insights.mjs` both filter `published=eq.true`, so fa drops out of
  the sitemap AND out of the hreflang cluster automatically. Without it the
  site would advertise a URL that redirects.

Targets: the two general guides go to the fa pillar. The Salalah one goes to
`/fa/buy-property-in-salalah`, **not** the pillar, because a redirect to a
topically unrelated page is treated as a soft 404.

⚠️ **`vercel.json` is validated against a strict schema and rejects a
`_comment` key.** That is why this reasoning lives here and not inline.

**Deliberately left alone, do not "finish the job" by removing these:**
- id 130, the fa "from Britain and Europe" guide. Real Persian audience: the
  Iranian diaspora in London, the same logic as the UAE landing.
- id 39, the general "for foreigners" ITC-rules page. Serves Iranians too, and
  it was already flagged in `HANDOFF-2026-08-24.md` as a useful page.

🆕 **A fourth fa page now carries the exact phrase in its title: id 174**, the
article published earlier the same day («بهترین سایت خرید ملک در عمان»). Its
intent is different and the «بهترین سایت» modifier separates it, but it belongs
on the watch list. If the pillar is still displaced in 3 weeks, check this one
in GSC before assuming the redirects failed.

Verified on prod: three fa URLs 308 to a live 200, en/ar/ru siblings still 200,
sitemap 652 URLs with zero fa entries and zero fa hreflang for those slugs.

## 3. Things a future session will get wrong here

- **The entity page's `agencyJsonLd()` deliberately shares `@id` with the
  site-wide organization node** and deliberately omits `url`. That makes it an
  ADDITION to the entity Google already knows rather than a rival entity.
  It uses `mainEntityOfPage` instead. Do not "fix" this by giving it its own
  @id or its own url.
- **`src/data/persianAgencyContent.mjs` has a claims allowlist in its header.**
  This page is about us, so a wrong sentence is worse here than anywhere else.
  No founding year, no employee count, no transaction volume, no awards, no
  "best in Oman", no yield. The licence number is allowed because the footer
  already publishes it in all four languages.
- **Three files now carry the entry price and unit count**:
  `iraniansUaeContent.mjs`, `persianAgencyContent.mjs` and `public/llms.txt`,
  on top of `seoRoutes.mjs`. All four move together in a sweep.
- **The vercel.json allowlist.** Unchanged rule, but it bit again: a `<Route>`
  without its rewrite is a production 404.

2. Re-run the original AI Mode query in 2 to 4 weeks and check whether section
