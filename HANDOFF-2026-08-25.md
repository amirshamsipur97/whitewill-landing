## 1. This session: being found by Google's AI Mode, not just by Google

The owner sent a screenshot of the AI Mode answer for «خرید ملک در عمان». Read
it correctly before doing more work here, because the obvious reading is wrong.

**We were already winning the part that is hard.** The answer renders our /buy
page as a source card and attaches an "Irfan Investment Group" citation chip to
its most important paragraph, the Freehold and ITC warning. Google accepted this
site as the authority for the ownership rule.

**We lost the part that is easy.** Section 3 of that answer, «وب‌سایت‌های
تخصصی و کارگزاری‌های فارسی‌زبان», names Homelist, OmanMelk and Imtilak Global.
Not us.

🔑 **Root cause, verified by grep before any code was written: the phrase
«فارسی‌زبان» appeared ZERO times in `src/`.** Not once, in a site with a
complete Persian edition. A model cannot place a business in a category no text
claims it belongs to. We were legible as "a source of Oman property prices" and
illegible as "a Persian-speaking brokerage".

This is an entity problem, not a crawl, index or authority problem. Do not
attack it with more keyword pages.

## 2. What shipped

**`/fa/persian-speaking-real-estate-agency-oman`** (new, fa only). The entity
page. Its job is not to rank for a head term; it is to be the passage a
generative engine lifts when it needs one sentence saying what this company is.
The answer box, every FAQ answer and each office block are written to be
extractable standalone, with no pronoun that needs a previous sentence.
Built on the exact `langs: ['fa']` rails the UAE landing proved: seoRoutes
entry, **vercel.json rewrite**, api/sitemap entry, footerSeoLinks fa service
link, nav item under the existing «بر اساس خریدار» group, and a full static
shell in prerender-routes. Offices come from `branches.js` rather than retyped
so they cannot drift from the footer. `ContactCTA source="persian_agency"`.

**Article `behtarin-sayt-kharid-melk-oman-2026`** (id 174, fa only). The AI
section that omitted us is assembled from pages that are themselves lists, and
every such page currently belongs to a competitor. This one maps the four
categories of option (portal, local Omani brokerage, Persian migration site,
Persian-speaking brokerage based in Oman), names real players in each including
ours, and discloses in its second paragraph that a Muscat broker wrote it.
🚨 **Every competitor claim in it was verified before writing.** The Imtilak
note is checked and correct: the Persian portal at imtilak.net sells Turkish
stock from Istanbul, `imtilak.om` is the separate Oman arm. Do not add
competitor claims to this article from memory.

**`public/llms.txt`**. It was live, returning 200, and **wrong**: it advertised
an entry price of OMR 35,625 to every AI crawler, two refreshes stale, with a
July project mix. This is the file AI crawlers read as the summary of the
business, and it had been missed by both price sweeps. Rewritten from current
data, given a one-line identity in each of the four languages, and no
superseded figure is left in it even as an example. **It is now part of the
inventory-refresh sweep**, and its own footer says so.

**Organization JSON-LD in index.html**. It was better than expected already
(knowsLanguage carried fa, and a Tehran contact point existed). Added
`legalName`, the brokerage licence number that was already printed in every
footer, a Persian `alternateName`, the UAE in `areaServed`, and the Russian
desk as its own contactPoint.

**Footer blurb, all four languages.** It renders on every page, which made it
the highest-frequency self-description on the site, and it was a generic
adjective that also over-claimed: "across Oman and the UAE" while all inventory
is Omani. It now names the languages the desk works in.

## 3. Things a future session will get wrong here

- **The entity page's `agencyJsonLd()` deliberately shares `@id` with the
  site-wide organization node** and deliberately omits `url`. That makes it an
  ADDITION to the entity Google already knows rather than a rival entity.
  It uses `mainEntityOfPage` instead. Do not "fix" this by giving it its own
  @id or its own url.
- **`src/data/persianAgencyContent.mjs` has a claims allowlist in its header.**
  This page is about us, so a wrong sentence is worse here than anywhere else.
  No founding year, no employee count, no transaction volume, no awards, no
  "best in Oman", no yield. The licence number is allowed because the footer
  already publishes it in all four languages.
- **Three files now carry the entry price and unit count**:
  `iraniansUaeContent.mjs`, `persianAgencyContent.mjs` and `public/llms.txt`,
  on top of `seoRoutes.mjs`. All four move together in a sweep.
- **The vercel.json allowlist.** Unchanged rule, but it bit again: a `<Route>`
  without its rewrite is a production 404.

## 4. How to judge whether this worked

Not by re-running the query and eyeballing the SERP, and not this week.

1. GSC → Performance → filter query «مشاور املاک فارسی‌زبان در عمان» and
   «بهترین سایت خرید ملک در عمان», then look at Pages.
2. Re-run the original AI Mode query in 2 to 4 weeks and check whether section
   3 names us. AI Mode retrieval lags the index.
3. Request Indexing for both new URLs. It is the only real lever on recrawl
   speed and it needs the owner.
