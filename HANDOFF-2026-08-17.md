### SEO

Three fixes from an audit of the live site, all deployed and verified:

1. **Real 404s.** `vercel.json` no longer rewrites everything to `/app.html`.
   It allowlists the 22 route shapes `App.jsx` serves, with and without a
   language prefix, and everything else goes to `api/not-found.js`.
   🚨 **Adding a `<Route>` now means adding its rewrite too, or the new page
   404s in production.** `/terms` this session is the worked example: App.jsx,
   seoRoutes.mjs, the footer, and two vercel.json entries.
2. **Sold units 301 to their project page** via `api/unit-status.js`. Vercel
   checks the filesystem before rewrites, so available units serve their
   prerendered page and never reach the function; only ids with no page do.
   Unknown ids get 410. 220 URLs came out of soft-404 state.
3. **Titles and descriptions.** 17 descriptions ran 165 to 263 characters and
   were rewritten, hook first, in all 4 languages. `/buy` and the three city
   landings now carry the price inside the first 60 characters with the head
   keyword still in position one.

Also: **project pages now share their own cover** as `og:image` at an unhashed
`/images/og/<slug>.<ext>`, instead of the site-wide peninsula.jpg. Nine of
twelve projects have a gallery and got a real share card. Unhashed on purpose,
because WhatsApp and the social platforms cache previews by URL.
